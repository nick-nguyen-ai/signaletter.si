    verdict: Worth reading
    what_changed: Anthropic's Frontier Red Team released FlashProve on 2 Nov under Apache-2.0. It uses zkSNARKs (short cryptographic proofs that show a computation ran correctly and reveal nothing else). A provider commits to a model's weights once, then proves each reply came from that model. On Llama 4 Maverick (400B parameters, 4-bit), one H100 generated proofs at about 110 tokens a second, roughly 70 to 95 times the GPU time of serving with vLLM; each proof is about 19MB and takes 17 to 21 seconds to verify on a CPU. Security properties are stated and proved in Lean, a formal proof language.
    why_it_matters: If a vendor or router claims you got a specific model, this is a path to checking it. That matters for model-risk validation and for regulators who want proof of which model was used.
    caveats: 'Research prototype: contexts up to 1,024 tokens, greedy decoding only, mixture-of-experts models only, and the fast C++ verifier has no formal guarantee.'
